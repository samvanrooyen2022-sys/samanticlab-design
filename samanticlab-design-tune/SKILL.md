# Tune: bolder, quieter, simpler

Read `samanticlab-design` first. Read the project's `<project>-direction.md` if it exists; tuning never changes the chosen direction, only how hard it is played.

## The three dials, and which one you are turning

They pull against each other, so name the dial before touching anything. One dial per pass. If the user asks for two ("bolder but calmer"), ask which comes first, do that, show it, then the other.

- **Bolder** = raise the amplitude of **one** target. Bigger, denser, more committed. The rest of the page stays as it is, or gets quieter to make room.
- **Quieter** = lower the amplitude **everywhere**. Less contrast between elements, fewer competing signals, softer weights. Nothing removed.
- **Simpler** = lower the **count**. Fewer elements, fewer words, fewer options. Amplitude stays; there is just less of it.

Before any pass, write one sentence of what is actually on screen: the real colours, the loaded faces, the layout topology. The pass happens inside that identity.

## Bolder

- The reflex answer, more effects, is the opposite of bold. Reject it first. Bold is commitment, not decoration.
- Pick the one target the user pointed at (a hero, a section, a heading). Raise it: type scale up, colour committed at region scale, one image at the scale it has in life, denser copy or emptier space, whichever the direction calls for.
- If every element got louder, the section got flatter. Bold needs a quiet neighbour.
- The skeleton test: strip the copy out. If the section only works once the words return, the boldness is in the text size, not the design. Rework the structure.
- Keep content true. A bolder claim is not a bolder design. Ask for real evidence if the section needs it; never invent it.
- A placeholder for an image names a job ("the workshop at 06:00, wide"), not a cue to drop in a decorative stock photo.
- The result is unmistakably the same brand, only more sure of itself.

## Quieter

- Quiet without intent collapses to generic. Decide what should still be loud (usually the one action and the one headline) and protect it.
- Reduce weights one step (900 to 700, 700 to 500). Reduce type-scale jumps but keep them obvious; the scale steps in the main file are the floor.
- Tinted greys, never pure grey. Never grey on colour.
- Fewer competing hues: keep the palette, lower the saturation of supporting colours or the area they cover.
- Motion: shorter distances (10 to 20px instead of 40), one easing, nothing continuous.
- Never: remove all colour (quiet is not greyscale), make everything small and light, add whitespace until the page floats apart. Quiet is precision, not absence.

## Simpler

- Copy first. Cut every sentence in half, then do it again. Remove hedging, marketing fluff, legalese, headings that restate the intro. Say it once.
- One primary action per screen. Group or cut the rest.
- Cards are not needed for basic layout; use spacing and alignment. Never cards inside cards.
- Pick left or centre alignment for the page and stick with it.
- Cut sections that repeat what another section says. Cut options nobody asked for.
- Keep the display face and the type scale. Simpler is fewer things, not smaller things. Body stays at 65 to 75 characters; "use the full width" makes prose unreadable.
- Do not remove the one authored moment or the signature interaction. Those are the point.

## After any pass

Run the checker on the changed file. Take the phone screenshot. Report under one word: fix or ship. Say exactly where to look to see the change.
